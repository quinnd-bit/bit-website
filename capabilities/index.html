Research standards

A record you
can follow.

We make the development and review of methods part of the evidence behind a finding.

Each method has a distinct edition. Its provenance chain records the source materials, transformations, analytical decisions, and outputs. When a method changes, the new edition and the reason for the change can be compared with what came before.

Methods go through successive rounds of challenge with stakeholders and subject-matter experts. Questions, feedback, revisions, and remaining limitations inform the work before findings are published.

Evidence across places

Local detail.
Shared questions.

Built-environment evidence spans buildings, climate, policy, and everyday life.

We bring earth observation, building information, scientific research, policy, and local knowledge together to understand how places change. City foundation models are one developing way to connect these sources and reveal patterns across a particular city.

Our global and country-level indicators provide comparable evidence across jurisdictions. For both, the sources, methods, assumptions, and limits matter as much as the result.

Explore Mariposa

Experimental / Kitchener–Waterloo

Learning from
one place.

Mariposa is our experimental city foundation model, grounded in Kitchener–Waterloo.

It gives us a place to explore how locally designed evidence can connect buildings, environment, and everyday life. Its methods and findings remain part of an ongoing research effort.

Research infrastructure

Tools in service
of the evidence.

Our internal research systems help analysts organize knowledge, develop methods, and preserve evidence and version history. They support the work; the published research is judged by its sources, methods, and review.

An early building-energy analysis pilot is also in development, combining building observations with wider environmental evidence. Its performance and applications are still being evaluated.
