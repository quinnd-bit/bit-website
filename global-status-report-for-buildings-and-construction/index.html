The research

Tracking the transition.
Informing the response.

Published by the UN Environment Programme and the Global Alliance for Buildings and Construction, the Global Status Report assesses the sector’s progress towards climate goals.

It brings together evidence on energy, emissions, policy, finance, technologies and solutions. That shared perspective helps governments, industry and researchers understand where progress is being made and where further action is needed.

The 2025–2026 report credits Ian Hamilton, Harry Kennard, Shih-Che Hsu, Lucien Hua, and Merve Yılmaz from Building Insights among its lead coordinating authors, alongside colleagues at the Buildings Performance Institute Europe.

From research to capability

Global measurement.
Local understanding.

Working across countries and datasets makes the value of consistent methods clear. It also reveals how much depends on the conditions of individual places.

That experience informs BIT’s direction: city foundation models designed around local evidence, alongside global and country-level indicators served through Core.

Over time, a portfolio of deeply observed cities can provide valuable reference evidence for testing and improving larger-scale measurement.
