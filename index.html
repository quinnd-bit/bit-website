Our purpose and principles
01 /

Lasting public value

A more sustainable built environment is the purpose of our work. We consider carbon, resilience, affordability, and wellbeing together, so better evidence can support decisions with lasting value.

02 /

Respect for place

A city is lived as well as measured. Its physical environment, social fabric, and policy context matter. Local knowledge helps us understand what the data alone cannot tell us.

03 /

Evidence you can examine

Understanding should stand up to scrutiny. We preserve sources and methods, make assumptions and limitations visible, and keep the path from evidence to insight traceable.

04 /

Progress together

Good decisions need different perspectives. Collaboration and informed human judgment guide our work. We share knowledge wherever possible and learn from the people applying it.

05 / Research standards

Research built to
stand up to scrutiny.

How our methods are developed

Reliable findings depend on methods that can be inspected, compared, and challenged by the people who know a place and a field best.

01 /

Provenance through the chain

We connect findings to their source materials, transformations, analytical decisions, limitations, and published outputs so the path from evidence to insight can be followed.

02 /

Methods with editions

Methods are editioned. Changes and their rationale are recorded, making it possible to compare approaches and understand how results evolve.

03 /

Challenge tested in rounds

We put methods through successive rounds of challenge with stakeholders and subject-matter experts, using their questions and feedback to refine the work.
