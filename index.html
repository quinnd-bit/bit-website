OUR PURPOSE

We generate the insights that shape a more sustainable built environment.

Building Insights combines rigorous research, evidence-based analysis, and strategic collaboration to help governments, industry, and communities make better decisions. By turning complex data into practical knowledge and tools, we support the transition toward buildings and construction systems that are more energy efficient, low carbon, climate resilient, and socially equitable.

Our Core Platforms: Transforming Built Environment Analytics

Building on our foundation of global research, Building Insights Together develops dedicated software products and APIs that serve enterprise asset managers, financial institutions, and policy authorities.

Decision OS & Institutional Memory

Forge Platform

An institutional decision engine and memory ledger designed for built-environment teams. Provides OECD/JRC-standard indicator governance, automated multi-agent research pipelines, and cryptographic evidence provenance.

Foundational Models & Vectors

Core Platform

Continuous spatial intelligence and physical asset representations. Powers our Global Gross Floor Area (GFA) nowcast engine (Indicator 1.1) and 256-D reusable non-residential building shell latent vectors.

Capital Markets & Transition Risk

Financial Solutions
