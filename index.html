Heritage & Lineage

From global advisory consulting to automated team products.

Building Insights Together was born from high-impact international consulting and academic leadership led by Professor Ian Hamilton (UCL Energy Institute) and collaborators across the UN Environment Programme (UNEP), International Energy Agency (IEA), and the Global Alliance for Buildings and Construction (GlobalABC).

While our past advisory work shaped seminal publications like the Global Status Report for Buildings and Construction, enterprise decarbonization requires continuous, automated, reproducible computation. We turned years of bespoke advisory methodologies into Core and Forge—transforming static reports into living software infrastructure.
