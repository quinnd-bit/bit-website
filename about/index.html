About Building Insights

Evidence, at the scale the built environment demands.

Building Insights Together Ltd generates the research, data, and practical tools that help governments, industry, and communities build a more sustainable future.

Our Story

Founded on a simple premise

That better data leads to better buildings and better buildings lead to better lives.

 

Building Insights Together began as a small working group of researchers and practitioners who kept running into the same problem: governments and developers wanted to build more sustainably, but the evidence they needed to act with confidence was scattered, inconsistent, or locked away in academic journals no one outside a university would read.

So we set out to close that gap — translating rigorous research into practical tools that planning departments, contractors, and community boards could actually use. What started as a handful of pilot studies on energy performance has grown into a standing research and advisory practice working across the built environment, from retrofit policy to climate-resilient infrastructure.

We’re still the same organization at heart: a group that believes the built environment is one of the most powerful levers we have for social and environmental change — and that it deserves the same evidentiary rigor as public health or economic policy.

“We generate the insights that shape a more sustainable built environment.”

Governance & Approach

How We Work, And What Keeps Us Honest

Building Insights Together is committed to achieving its objectives through responsible actions, measurable progress and continuous improvement. Four principles govern every project we take on — select any one to see what it means in practice.

Rigorous Research And Analysis

Grounded in credible data and internationally recognised methods.

We declare our methods before we start and retain the underlying data afterwards, so any finding we publish can be checked by someone who did not produce it.

  • Internationally recognised measurement and reporting standards
  • Primary data collection wherever secondary data cannot be verified
  • Assumptions and limitations stated on the face of every output
  • Findings published whether or not they suit the commissioning party

Collaborative Partnerships

With academic institutions, governments, international organisations, industry and civil society.

The evidence gap is too wide for any one organisation to close. We build long-term working relationships rather than one-off commissions, and we name our funders on the work they funded.

  • Joint research programmes with universities and research institutes
  • Advisory work with public bodies and international organisations
  • Every engagement screened for conflict of interest before it begins
  • Community and civil society partners involved from design, not at review

Open Knowledge Sharing

Sharing our knowledge and insights to maximise the reach and usefulness of our research.

Research that sits behind a paywall cannot change a decision. We publish openly by default and translate findings into the format the audience actually uses.

  • Open-access publication as the standard, not the exception
  • Tools, datasets and calculators released for public use
  • Plain-language summaries alongside every technical report
  • Training and capacity building for the teams applying the work

Responsible Innovation

Ensuring new tools and analytical approaches are developed with transparency, integrity and sustainability in mind.

New methods earn their place by being testable. Anything we build — model, tool or index — ships with its assumptions visible and its failure modes documented.

  • Models validated against measured outcomes before release
  • Documented limitations, uncertainty ranges and appropriate use
  • Data handled to recognised privacy and security standards
  • Our own operational footprint held to the standard we recommend

Mission & Vision

Building a Better Future

We’re committed to shaping a built environment that supports people, strengthens communities, and creates lasting environmental, social, and economic impact through evidence-based action.

Our Mission

To build understanding, generate insights and knowledge, and create tools that help society design and deliver a more sustainable built environment.

We combine rigorous research, evidence-based analysis and strategic collaboration to help governments, industry and communities make better decisions. Turning complex data into practical knowledge and tools is not a by-product of the work. It is the work.

Our Mission

A more sustainable, resilient, and inclusive built environment begins with informed decisions backed by credible evidence.

The choices we make today shape the communities of tomorrow. Building Insights works with organizations and communities to provide transparent research and practical strategies that create lasting environmental and social impact.
