Origin & Scientific Lineage

Grounded in world-class research.
Engineered for software scale.

Building Insights Together was created to address a systemic failure in the built environment: critical climate and energy decisions were being made on unvalidated estimates, fragmented spreadsheets, and static academic reports.

Our Lineage

Emerging from global advisory consulting.

Our team’s origins are rooted in high-level consulting and research partnerships led by Professor Ian Hamilton (UCL Energy Institute) in collaboration with international bodies including the United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP), International Energy Agency (IEA), and the Global Alliance for Buildings and Construction (GlobalABC).

For over a decade, our predecessors delivered analytical authorship and advisory frameworks for the flagship Global Status Report for Buildings and Construction, tracking worldwide energy intensities, floor area additions, and decarbonization gaps across 190+ countries.

However, consulting reports only provide static snapshots every one to two years. As the transition accelerated, financial institutions, infrastructure funds, and enterprises required continuous, reproducible, and verifiable computation. Building Insights Together was founded to turn that consultative expertise into production software products.

Core Distinction

From Reports to Software Infrastructure

Instead of one-off advisory engagements, we develop automated pipelines. We combine remote sensing (Copernicus Sentinel, Landsat, USGS 3DEP LiDAR), machine learning transformers, and geospatial parcel databases into continuous data APIs.

Core Distinction

Zero-Fabrication Rigor

In an era of opaque AI models, every metric generated by Building Insights Together is audited, reproducible, and tied directly to physical ground truth and verifiable data receipts.

Enterprise Products

The BIT Product Architecture

How we deliver verifiable intelligence to capital markets and enterprise partners.

PRODUCT: FORGE

Decision Operating System

Forge is our internal and enterprise decision platform. It functions as an institutional ledger where research findings, evaluation experiments, and policy decisions are tracked with cryptographic SHA-256 integrity.

  • Attributable institutional memory & immutable audit trails
  • Autonomous multi-agent research and calibration workers
  • Continuous evidence gates across multi-indicator portfolios
  • OECD/JRC 10-step composite indicator governance
PRODUCT: CORE

Foundational Asset Models & APIs

Core is our foundational data engine. It transforms raw physical observations, Earth-observation imagery, and cadastral records into unified mathematical representations of building stocks.

  • Global Gross Floor Area (GFA) nowcast engine (Indicator 1.1)
  • 256-D reusable non-residential building shell embeddings
  • Audit-grade PCAF Scope 3 Cat 15 financed emissions calculation
  • CRREM stranding curves & asset-level transition pathways
