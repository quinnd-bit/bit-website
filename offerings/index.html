Offerings

A richer understanding.
A wider field of action.

Better public decisions. More resilient buildings. A richer understanding of home. We are developing city foundation models to support these aims across government, climate retrofit, insurance, and real estate.

These applications describe our development direction. Each engagement begins with the place, the question and the evidence available.

Government Climate retrofit Insurance Real estate

01 / Government

Understand the city.
Focus the response.

Bring local evidence into the decisions that shape public life.

Our experience in international buildings research informs a locally grounded approach to public decisions. Connect buildings, infrastructure, administrative records, and policy to identify priorities and work across departments, with local needs in view.

  • Explore patterns across neighbourhoods and public services
  • Inform planning, climate adaptation and public investment
  • Develop programmes with a traceable evidence base

02 / Climate retrofit

Find where change
can matter most.

Use Helios to connect interior detail, exterior conditions, and neighbourhood context.

Helios combines on-device interior feature extraction, a physics engine, and exterior indicators from Core to provide a privacy-protecting, 360° view of a building. It can work with a city foundation model or broader indicators to explore retrofit opportunities and support programme design.

  • Use on-device ML to extract useful features from interior LiDAR observations
  • Connect Core indicators to a building physics engine for energy analysis
  • Inform retrofit planning and investment priorities at building or city scale

Helios is in development and pilot testing. Explore Helios

03 / Insurance

Put property in
its wider context.

Bring richer local evidence to property and portfolio analysis.

Explore how building characteristics, surrounding conditions and climate exposure relate across a place. City foundation models offer a way to connect these sources and support more informed assessment and resilience planning.

  • Enrich property assessment with neighbourhood evidence
  • Explore shared conditions across a portfolio
  • Investigate resilience measures with sources in view

04 / Real estate

See the place
beyond the property.

Explore a neighbourhood through the things that matter to you.

A home is part of a larger place. Help buyers, owners and investors investigate the connections between properties, daily life and the surrounding environment—from green space and everyday access to building characteristics and climate context.

  • Discover neighbourhoods through personal interests
  • Connect property information with the surrounding area
  • Bring local context into investment and ownership decisions
